# Executive Dashboard Wireframe

## Overview
The Executive Dashboard provides high-level KPI visibility for C-suite executives and senior leadership, with focus on strategic metrics and business health indicators.

## Layout Structure

### Header Section
- Company Logo (Top Left)
- Dashboard Title: "Executive Dashboard"
- Date Range Selector (Top Right)
- Refresh Button
- Navigation Menu Toggle

### Main Content Area

#### Row 1: Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Revenue (Card with trend indicator)
- Net Profit Margin (Card with trend indicator)
- Customer Satisfaction Score (Card with trend indicator)
- Market Share (Card with trend indicator)

#### Row 2: Strategic Metrics
- Revenue Growth Chart (Line Chart - 12 months)
- Profitability Trend (Bar Chart - Quarterly)
- Market Position (Gauge Chart)
- Growth Forecast (Trend Chart)

#### Row 3: Business Health
- Department Performance Matrix
- Regional Sales Distribution (Map)
- Customer Retention Rate
- Operational Efficiency Index

#### Row 4: Alerts & Actions
- Critical Alerts Panel
- Recommended Actions
- Executive Notifications

### Footer
- Last Updated Timestamp
- Data Source Attribution
- Export/Share Options

## Design Notes
- Responsive layout for desktop and tablet
- Dark theme with accent colors for alerts
- Priority: Scannable, high-impact visuals
- All KPIs include YoY comparison indicators
