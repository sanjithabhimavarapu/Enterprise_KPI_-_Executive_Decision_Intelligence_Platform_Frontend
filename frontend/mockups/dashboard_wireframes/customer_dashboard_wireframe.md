# Customer Dashboard Wireframe

## Overview
The Customer Dashboard tracks customer metrics, satisfaction, and engagement for sales, marketing, and customer success teams.

## Layout Structure

### Header Section
- Dashboard Title: "Customer Analytics Dashboard"
- Customer Segment Filter
- Date Range Selector
- Cohort Analysis Toggle

### Main Content Area

#### Row 1: Customer Metrics
- Total Customers (KPI Card)
- New Customer Acquisitions (KPI Card with growth %)
- Customer Churn Rate (KPI Card)
- Lifetime Value (LTV) (KPI Card)

#### Row 2: Engagement Analysis
- Customer Satisfaction Score (NPS) (Gauge)
- Retention Rate (Progress Bar)
- Customer Health Score Distribution (Donut Chart)
- Support Ticket Resolution (SLA Compliance)

#### Row 3: Customer Segmentation
- Segment Performance Matrix
- Geographic Distribution Map
- Industry Breakdown
- Revenue by Customer Segment

#### Row 4: Trends & Predictions
- Customer Growth Trend (Line Chart)
- Churn Risk Alerts
- Upsell Opportunities
- Customer Sentiment Timeline

### Detailed Views
- Customer List with Filters
- Interaction History
- Purchase History
- Feedback/Reviews Summary

## Design Notes
- Segment-based filtering
- Real-time satisfaction metrics
- Predictive churn indicators
- Integration with CRM data
