# Governance Dashboard Wireframe

## Overview
The Governance Dashboard ensures compliance, risk management, and audit trail tracking for compliance officers and internal audit teams.

## Layout Structure

### Header Section
- Dashboard Title: "Governance & Compliance Dashboard"
- Compliance Status Indicator
- Regulation Filter (SOX, GDPR, HIPAA, etc.)
- Audit Period Selector

### Main Content Area

#### Row 1: Compliance Status
- Overall Compliance Score (Gauge)
- Critical Issues Count (Alert Card)
- Pending Approvals (Counter)
- Policy Violations (Alert Card)

#### Row 2: Risk Assessment
- Risk Heat Map (Risk vs Impact Matrix)
- Risk Trend Analysis (Line Chart)
- Mitigation Status (Progress Tracking)
- Control Effectiveness Score

#### Row 3: Audit & Controls
- Active Audit Schedule (Timeline)
- Control Assessment Status (Status Table)
- Remediation Actions (Open Items)
- Change Management Log

#### Row 4: Compliance Tracking
- Regulation Compliance Status (All regulations)
- Certificate & License Expiration (Timeline)
- Training Completion Tracking
- Incident Log & Resolution

### Detailed Sections
- Policy Documentation Links
- Audit Reports Archive
- Compliance Calendar
- Risk Register

## Design Notes
- Strict access control and role-based views
- Complete audit trail logging
- Document version control
- Compliance report generation
- Alert escalation workflow
